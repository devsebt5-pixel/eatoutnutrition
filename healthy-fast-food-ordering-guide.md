## How to use this guide

Fast food isn't automatically unhealthy. The same Chipotle counter that serves a 1,200-calorie burrito will build a 530-calorie bowl with 73 grams of protein. The difference is the order, and the healthy version is almost never the default. This guide gives you the exact orders to ask for, the numbers behind them, and the handful of swaps that do most of the work.

### What counts as a healthy fast food order?

We judged every order on four things at once:

- **Calories:** a full meal at 650 or less. Most picks here land under 500.
- **Protein:** 25g or more where the menu allows. Protein is what keeps you full until the next meal.
- **Sodium:** as low as the menu realistically gets. The FDA daily value is 2,300mg, and one meal can use most of it.
- **Orderable as written:** no secret-menu hacks. Say the build at the counter and it works.

### Where the numbers come from

Chipotle, Jersey Mike's and Five Guys figures are calculated from each chain's own published per-ingredient nutrition data. This is the same data behind the free [Chipotle](https://eatoutnutrition.com/chipotle-nutrition-calculator/), [Jersey Mike's](https://eatoutnutrition.com/jersey-mikes-nutrition-calculator/) and [Five Guys](https://eatoutnutrition.com/five-guys-nutrition-calculator/) calculators on EatOutNutrition.

Figures for the other chains come from each brand's published nutrition information as of September 2026. Food is assembled by hand and menus change, so treat every number as a close estimate.

## 7 rules for ordering healthy at any fast food place

These work at every chain in this guide, and at the ones that aren't.

1. **Start with the protein.** Pick grilled chicken, turkey or lean beef first and build around it. Aim for 25g or more.
2. **Shrink or skip the bread.** Bowls, tubs, lettuce wraps, 6-inches, Skinnys and Little burgers all cut 200–320 calories without touching the protein.
3. **Watch the fats on your food.** Oil, mayo, creamy sauces and dressings are the most calorie-dense things on the counter. Mustard, salsa, hot sauce and vinegar are nearly free.
4. **Load up on free vegetables.** Lettuce, tomato, onions, peppers, mushrooms and fajita veggies add volume and fiber for almost nothing.
5. **Rethink the side.** Swap fries for fruit, a side salad or nothing. Even Little fries at Five Guys are 526 calories.
6. **Drink zero calories.** Choose water, unsweetened tea, diet soda or black coffee. A large regular soda can outweigh your burger.
7. **Pick one salty thing.** Cured meats, cheese, bread, pickles and many salsas all carry sodium. Choose the one you want and go light on the rest.

## Pocket checklist

Screenshot or print this, and run through it before you order.

- [ ] Does my order have a real protein (25g+)?
- [ ] Can I get it as a bowl, tub, wrap or smaller size?
- [ ] Are sauces and dressing on the side, or swapped for salsa or mustard?
- [ ] Did I add the free vegetables?
- [ ] Did I skip the fries, or share the smallest size?
- [ ] Is my drink zero-calorie?
- [ ] Have I checked the sodium, not just the calories?

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming "salad" means healthy.** Dressing can add more calories, sugar and sodium than the salad itself.
- **Sizing up for value.** A regular Jersey Mike's sub made Mike's Way runs 820–1,160 calories. Smaller sizes are usually the single biggest cut.
- **Ignoring sodium.** Plenty of 400-calorie orders carry 1,500mg+ of sodium.
- **Cutting protein to save calories.** A 250-calorie lunch with 8g of protein often leads to a 400-calorie snack by mid-afternoon.
